## Frozen experiment

Source commit: `0a798d0341bf9ca662dcc9218b24e242b1ac1705`.
Configuration: [browser-adaptation-pilot.json](../configs/browser-adaptation-pilot.json).
Run directory: `experiments/browser-adaptation/pilot-01/`.

The TCN warm-starts from B17 and keeps its normalization statistics, originally
fit on the 14,000 CESNET22 training flows. Those flows and the ten local training
connections supply cross-entropy updates: 75% public and 25% local batch mass,
with local classes balanced in expectation. The pilot uses five epochs, 50
steps each, batch size 64, learning rate 0.0001, seed 17 and two CPU threads.
Valid prefixes are sampled uniformly from 5/10/20/30; no augmentation or SupCon
is used in this update. The existing compact architecture is unchanged.

The forest uses the same underlying training flows and all four prefix budgets,
with exactly the same domain/class weight masses. It uses 32 trees of depth at
most 12 with minimum leaf size 2. This bounded configuration fits available
laptop memory. No new architecture or destination/process feature was added.
Every inference path still consumes only packet sizes, directions and gaps.

Selection uses the second visits exclusively for local checks and a fixed
first-50-per-class sample of the original public selection partition for a
historical retention control. No source final-test records or calibration data
were opened. The historical 2022 control predates browser training in 2026; it
is not a forward generalization test. Predeclared eligibility requires local
mean-budget observed-class macro-F1 to improve by at least 0.10, no local-class
recall regression, and public clean mean macro-F1 loss no greater than 0.02.

The candidate weights, normalization and choice are now frozen. Next are
separate calibration recordings, followed by a separately frozen policy and
fresh user-started live evaluation. Calibration requires at least 30 accepted
local prefix points, support from both services and the predeclared empirical
error limits. More recordings may be needed for support; no infeasible policy
will silently start committing labels. Thresholds will not be fit on the second
visits or final sessions. The app remains on B17 during calibration collection.

The first separate calibration recording, `instagram-cal-01`, now has seven
matched connections (six at 30 packets and one at 16), giving 26 available
prefix points. All 196 payload sizes match the browser sequence within direction;
all 150 saved live B17 predictions reproduce exactly. Its role is calibration
only, and no threshold has been fitted while `youtube-cal-01` is missing.

The implemented joint policy uses the frozen 0.00–1.00 threshold grid in 0.01
steps. It requires at least 30 accepted local prefix points, at least five from
each local service, empirical accepted error ≤0.10 overall and per local service,
and ≤0.10 on a separate historical public-calibration control with at least
30 accepted points. It maximizes equally weighted local/public coverage under
those constraints. Temperature stays 1 and minimum length stays five packets.
Infeasible policies remain unfitted. Extra reserved calibration sessions require
a prior infeasible report; development/final recordings cannot be substituted.
These empirical constraints on correlated points are not population guarantees.
